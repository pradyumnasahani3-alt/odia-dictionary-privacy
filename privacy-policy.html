Privacy Policy for English To Odia Dictionary

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the English To Odia Dictionary mobile application (the "App") developed by Pradyumna Sahani (the "Developer"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

The English To Odia Dictionary app does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. The app operates entirely offline using a local database stored on your device.

Automatically Collected Information

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Favorite Words: Words you mark as favorites are stored locally
  • Quiz Progress: Your quiz scores and history are stored locally
  • Search History: Recent searches may be cached locally for performance
Important: None of this data is transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information to provide personalized ads. This includes:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data
  • Location data (approximate, based on IP address)

AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Data Storage and Security

All user-generated data (favorites, quiz scores) is stored locally on your device using:

  • Local storage (browser storage API)
  • SQLite database (for dictionary data)

We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted over the internet.

Children's Privacy

This app does NOT knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed for general audiences and contains educational content suitable for all ages.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your data: Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data
  • Opt out of ads: Use your device settings to limit ad tracking
  • Access your data: All data is stored locally on your device
